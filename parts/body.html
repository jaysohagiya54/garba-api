What these prices are

The pass rate for each category, its name, whether it’s sold out, and how many people it admits — all taken from the platforms’ own listing data. No estimates anywhere on this page.

Official pass rate+Platform fees at checkout=Your final total

Neither platform publishes its convenience fee before login, so the final total is whatever the checkout screen shows. Compare here, pay there.

Kept current

District listings refresh automatically twice a day from the platform’s own pricing API, including each ticket’s published convenience fee and GST. BookMyShow listings come from its public listing data.

The page pulls the latest figures every time it loads. Every card links straight to the official listing, so you go from a price here to that booking page in one tap.
